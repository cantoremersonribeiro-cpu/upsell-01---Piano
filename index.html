Your 5-Step Play-by-Ear Path
The same five steps for every simple song
1
Find the home noteHum the last note of the song. That is home, and it tells you which chords to use underneath.Easy
2
Find the first noteHum it and match it on the keys around middle C. It is usually C, E or G.Easy
3
Up, down or the same?Follow the direction of the melody, one note at a time. Your voice already knows the shape.Easy
4
Step or skip?Most melodies move to the very next key. When they jump, they usually land on a note of the chord.Moderate
5
Add your left handPick the chord that contains the melody note on beat 1, and play both hands together.Moderate
